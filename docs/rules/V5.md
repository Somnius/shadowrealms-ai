# V5 rules spec: Vampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition

What ShadowRealms AI needs to implement for the **V5** ruleset. Source: the V5 Corebook (the September 2019 errata update, which already folds in most of Errata 2.0) plus the separate **V5 Rules Errata 2.0** document (dated September 6, 2019). Machine-readable constants live in [`v5.json`](v5.json).

Page refs ("core p. N") are the printed corebook page numbers. Everything below is paraphrased. Where the errata changes something and the core text in hand already includes the change, that's noted. Where the errata document and the core text disagree, the conflict is flagged.

**AMBIGUOUS** means the text is unclear or contradicts itself. The app should expose a setting or a documented default.

## 1. Dice

### 1.1 Pools (core pp. 117–120)

- A pool is usually **Attribute + Skill**. It can also be **Attribute + Attribute** (e.g. Resolve + Composure to resist many Disciplines, or Strength + Strength for a pure lift) or **Attribute + Discipline**.
- If the character has no dots in the Skill, roll the Attribute alone. There is **no extra penalty** (p. 118).
- **Minimum pool is 1 die.** Penalties can't drop a pool below one die (p. 120). A tracker roll on an empty tracker still gets 1 die (p. 119).
- Rolls that use a tracker (Health, Willpower) use the **current undamaged** value, not the full rating (p. 119). Humanity is not a tracker pool: a Humanity roll uses the rating; Stains only matter for Remorse.
- ST-imposed modifiers come in two forms: add or remove dice, or change the Difficulty. As a rule of thumb, 2 dice ≈ 1 Difficulty. Ad-hoc modifiers should be capped at ±2 Difficulty or ±3 dice. This cap doesn't apply to specialties or modifiers set by specific rules (p. 120).

### 1.2 Success and Difficulty (core pp. 118–121)

- Every die showing **6–10 is a success**. The per-die target is **always 6**.
- **Difficulty = the number of successes needed** (this is the big change from classic). You win if successes ≥ Difficulty.
- Difficulty table (p. 119):

| Task | Difficulty |
|---|---|
| Routine | 1 |
| Straightforward | 2 |
| Moderate | 3 |
| Challenging | 4 |
| Hard | 5 |
| Very Hard | 6 |
| Nearly Impossible | 7+ |

- The errata fixes the wording to "table below" (p. 119). The core text in hand already reads that way.
- For opposition you don't want to roll, the Difficulty can be set three ways: from the table, as **half the opponent's pool rounded down**, or as the opponent's Skill rating (minimum 1) (p. 119).
- **Margin** = successes − Difficulty. Damage and many powers scale with the margin (p. 121).
- **Automatic win** (ST option): if the pool is **at least twice the Difficulty**, the ST can skip the roll. The margin is then 0. Rarely used in combat (p. 120). It never applies to checks (p. 123) or to rolls boosted by a Blood Surge (p. 218).
- **Win at a cost**: if the roll has *some* successes but fails, the ST may offer a win with a complication. The player can say no and take the failure (p. 121).
- **Total failure**: **zero successes**. The consequences are up to the ST (p. 122).

### 1.3 Criticals (core pp. 120–121)

- Every **pair of 10s** (regular or Hunger dice) is a critical. A pair counts **4 successes** instead of 2.
- Formula: `successes = count(die ≥ 6) + 2 × floor(count(die == 10) / 2)`
  - Three 10s → 5 successes. Four 10s → 8 successes.
- A **critical win** is a *winning* roll that contains at least one pair. A critical on a roll that still fails isn't a critical win. See bestial failure below.

### 1.4 Hunger dice (core pp. 205–208)

- Hunger runs from **0 to 5**. When building a pool, **swap regular dice for Hunger dice one-for-one** up to the Hunger level. If the pool is smaller than Hunger, every die is a Hunger die. The pool size doesn't change (p. 205).
- **Never** put Hunger dice into **Rouse Checks, Willpower rolls or Humanity rolls** (p. 205). That includes frenzy resistance, which is a Willpower roll.
  - **AMBIGUOUS:** the Remorse roll (p. 239) is based on the Humanity track but the text doesn't explicitly call it a "Humanity roll". Default: no Hunger dice.
- Hunger dice succeed on 6+ like any other die and can be part of a pair of 10s.
- **Messy critical** (p. 207): a **critical win** where at least one of the 10s is on a Hunger die. For example, one regular 10 plus one Hunger 10 is messy. Two regular 10s plus a third 10 on a Hunger die: the text says "one or more 10s appears on a Hunger die", so it's messy as written.
  - It succeeds, but the Beast's way. Typical results: Stains, a Masquerade breach, losing an Advantage dot. If none of those fit, the mess turns the result into a failure.
- **Bestial failure** (p. 207): a **failed** roll (didn't reach the Difficulty, or lost the contest) with **at least one Hunger die showing 1**.
  - A successful roll can **never** be a bestial failure, however many Hunger 1s it shows.
  - Typical result: a Compulsion. Gentler options: lose an Advantage dot, take Aggravated Health damage, or **+1 Hunger**. If that pushes Hunger above 5, the vampire rolls to resist **hunger frenzy at Difficulty 4**.
  - A total failure with a Hunger 1 is both a total failure and a bestial failure.
- Hunger can't go **below 0** or **above 5**. "Above 5" triggers the rules in §4.

### 1.5 Willpower re-roll (core p. 122; p. 158; nothing in Errata 2.0)

- Spend **1 Willpower** to **re-roll up to three regular (non-Hunger) dice** on one roll. The player picks which dice, successes included. Re-rolling regular 10s is the book's suggested way to defuse a messy critical (p. 205 sidebar).
- **Not allowed** on:
  - Hunger dice.
  - Checks, including Rouse Checks (p. 123).
  - Tracker rolls (Willpower, Humanity).
  - One-Roll Conflict (p. 158).
  - While in frenzy: spending Willpower to take control is allowed, re-rolls are not (p. 220).
- The spent point is marked as **Superficial Willpower damage**, and spend damage is **not halved**. If every box is already Superficial, one turns Aggravated (p. 126).
- Dice added by a Blood Surge stay in the pool through the re-roll (p. 218).
- **AMBIGUOUS:** the text says "up to three regular dice on any one roll". It never says whether a second Willpower point can be spent on the same roll. Default: **one Willpower re-roll per roll**.
- Other Willpower spends (p. 158):
  - Take control for one turn during frenzy or under Dominate/Presence-style coercion.
  - Twitch while staked.
  - Ignore Health penalties, including Impairment, for one turn.

### 1.6 Specialties (core pp. 118, 159)

- **+1 die** when the action falls under a specialty. **Only one specialty per roll.**
- A Skill can have at most as many specialties as it has dots, except **Craft**, which can have more.
- Academics, Craft, Performance and Science each come with **one free specialty** when the first dot is bought.
- Specialties can't be so broad that they apply to nearly every use of the Skill (no "Muay Thai" for Brawl).
- Cost: **3 XP** each.

### 1.7 Checks: Rouse and others (core pp. 123, 211)

- A **check** is a **single die, success on 6+**.
- No Willpower re-roll. Automatic wins and "taking half" don't apply.
- **Rouse Check**: success → Hunger stays the same. Failure → **Hunger +1**. The effect that called for the check always happens, pass or fail. The Hunger increase is applied after the effect (p. 211).
- Blood Potency can let the player roll **two dice and keep the higher** on Rouse Checks for Discipline powers up to a level set by BP (table in §3.4) (pp. 211, 216–217).
- **At Hunger 5:**
  - The vampire can't Rouse voluntarily.
  - If something forces a Rouse Check, the player makes an immediate **hunger frenzy test at Difficulty 4**, and the effect still happens (p. 211).
  - Exceptions: a failed **waking** Rouse, or a failed Rouse while mending Aggravated damage, that would push Hunger above 5 sends the vampire into **torpor** instead (pp. 219, 223).
- What costs a Rouse Check:
  - Waking each night.
  - Blush of Life.
  - Blood Surge.
  - Mending (1 Rouse per turn for Superficial; 3 Rouses at nightfall per Aggravated point).
  - Most Discipline powers (per power).
  - Several other powers.

### 1.8 Contests and conflicts (core pp. 123–127)

- **Contest:** both sides roll their pools. **The acting character wins ties**, since they win with successes ≥ the opponent's (p. 123).
- **Taking half:** for an SPC, the ST can treat floor(pool / 2) as its successes. This never applies to checks (p. 123).
- **Conflict** (anything that deals damage, physical or social; pp. 124–126):
  - Rolls are simultaneous.
  - The winner deals **(winner's successes − loser's successes) + weapon modifier** to the loser's Health (physical) or Willpower (social).
  - **Two-sided** conflict: both sides can hurt each other. On a tie, both deal damage as if with a margin of 1.
  - **One-sided** conflict: the defender can't deal damage.
  - **Ranged:** a contest against Dexterity + Athletics. No cover: −2 dice to defense. Strong cover: +1 or +2 (p. 125).
  - **Dodging** with Dexterity + Athletics instead of a combat Skill: a defender who wins deals no damage (p. 125).
  - **Multiple opponents:** −1 die to defense for each additional attacker. Attacking more than one target means splitting the pool (p. 125).
  - Order of resolution: close combat already engaged → ranged → newly started close combat → everything else. Ties go to Dexterity + Wits, then to the Skill used (p. 125).
- Weapon modifiers (p. 304):

| Weapon | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Improvised, stake | +0 |
| Light impact | +1 |
| Heavy impact, light piercing, light gunshot | +2 |
| Heavy melee, medium gunshot | +3 |
| Heavy gunshot, huge melee | +4 |

  - **AMBIGUOUS** due to table layout: rows with an empty value cell are assumed to share the next value shown. Verify against the PDF before relying on the +2/+3 assignments.
- **Teamwork** (p. 122): roll the largest pool among the helpers, +1 die for each helper with at least 1 dot in the Skill. If no Skill is involved, anyone can help.
- **Extended tests** (pp. 293–294), four variants:
  - **Standard:** accumulate successes toward a large Difficulty (10+).
  - **Series:** a set number of wins at a normal Difficulty.
  - **Hard:** only the margin counts toward the total.
  - **Cascading:** the margin carries over as bonus dice to the next roll; one failure ends the test.
  - A **total failure** in an extended test wipes the progress. The ST may forbid starting over.
  - **Extended contests:** first to the Difficulty wins. If both get there in the same increment, the higher total wins. A variant has each increment be a contest where only the winner banks its margin.

## 2. Character sheet (V5)

### 2.1 Attributes (9; p. 152)

| Physical | Social | Mental |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | Charisma | Intelligence |
| Dexterity | Manipulation | Wits |
| Stamina | Composure | Resolve |

Normally rated 1–5. 0 is possible and means a real handicap (p. 155).

### 2.2 Skills (27; pp. 152–153)

| Physical | Social | Mental |
|---|---|---|
| Athletics | Animal Ken | Academics |
| Brawl | Etiquette | Awareness |
| Craft | Insight | Finance |
| Drive | Intimidation | Investigation |
| Firearms | Leadership | Medicine |
| Larceny | Performance | Occult |
| Melee | Persuasion | Politics |
| Stealth | Streetwise | Science |
| Survival | Subterfuge | Technology |

Rated 0–5.

### 2.3 Disciplines (p. 153)

Animalism, Auspex, Blood Sorcery, Celerity, Dominate, Fortitude, Obfuscate, Potence, Presence, Protean, Thin-Blood Alchemy.

- One power is chosen per dot (p. 149).
- Only one Discipline power can be activated per turn, but any number can be running at once (p. 219).

### 2.4 Trackers (pp. 119, 126–127, 157–158)

- **Health = Stamina + 3.**
- **Willpower = Composure + Resolve.**
  - Willpower can't be bought directly, at creation or with XP. It only rises when Composure or Resolve rises (p. 157).
- Two damage types:
  - **Superficial**, marked "/".
  - **Aggravated**, marked "X".
- **Halving:** unless a rule says otherwise, Superficial damage is **halved, rounded up**, before it's applied (p. 126). The text puts this in the general damage rules, and the p. 304 weapon example halves it too. Read as written, it applies to every target, not only vampires.
  - Exceptions: Willpower spends, Feral Weapons (Errata 2.0), and some powers.
- What deals what (p. 126):
  - Fists and non-lethal weapons: Superficial to humans.
  - Standard weapons, including bullets and blades: Superficial to vampires.
  - Sharp or piercing weapons: Aggravated to humans.
  - Fire, sunlight, and the claws and teeth of some supernatural creatures: Aggravated to vampires.
  - **AMBIGUOUS:** the text doesn't say outright whether blunt weapons (bats) do Superficial or Aggravated damage to humans. Default: Superficial.
  - Social conflict damages Willpower. Revealing secrets, or attacks from close friends, deal Aggravated Willpower damage.
- **Impaired:** when every box on a tracker is filled, with any mix of damage types.
  - Impaired Health: −2 dice to Physical pools.
  - Impaired Willpower: −2 dice to Social and Mental pools.
  - Each further point of damage (after halving) turns one Superficial box into Aggravated (p. 127).
- When a tracker is **completely filled with Aggravated** damage:
  - Health: a mortal dies or is comatose. A vampire enters **torpor** (p. 127, p. 223).
  - Willpower: in a social conflict, the character breaks down. Outside one, it only means Impairment (p. 127).
- **Healing:**
  - Mortals:
    - Start of session: remove Superficial Health damage up to Stamina.
    - Aggravated Health: an Intelligence + Medicine test can convert Aggravated to Superficial. Difficulty = total Aggravated damage, +1 if treating oneself. At most half the Medicine rating (rounded up) can be converted per night. Hospital: total Aggravated damage in weeks (p. 127).
  - Vampires: Superficial Health is mended with Rouse Checks. Aggravated Health costs 3 Rouse Checks at nightfall per point (§3.6).
  - Willpower, everyone:
    - Start of session: remove Superficial Willpower damage up to the **higher** of Composure or Resolve (p. 158). The ST may withhold this after a cliffhanger.
    - Once per session, acting toward a **Desire** restores 1 Superficial.
    - Playing out a messy critical, bestial failure, frenzy or Compulsion well can restore 1 or more Superficial (ST call).
    - Aggravated Willpower: 1 point at the end of a session in which the character **worked toward their Ambition**. 1 or more for acting to benefit a Touchstone or uphold a Conviction against their own interest (pp. 158–159, 174).

### 2.5 Hunger, Blood Potency, Resonance

- **Hunger 0–5** (§1.4, §3).
- **Blood Potency 0–10.** It's bounded by generation (table in §3.4) and grants the effects in the BP table. Player characters normally have BP 0–3 (p. 217).
- **Resonance** (pp. 226–228) comes from the vessel's emotional state:

| Resonance | Disciplines boosted |
|---|---|
| Choleric | Celerity, Potence |
| Melancholy | Fortitude, Obfuscate |
| Phlegmatic | Auspex, Dominate |
| Sanguine | Blood Sorcery, Presence |
| Animal blood | Animalism, Protean |

- Each Resonance comes with a temperament:
  - **Fleeting:** no mechanical effect (besides thin-blood alchemy).
  - **Intense:** **+1 die** to pools for the matching Disciplines until the next feeding or until Hunger hits 5.
  - **Acute:** the same bonus plus a **Dyscrasia**. Its effects usually last until the next feeding or Hunger 5. It typically requires killing the vessel, or feeding on it across three nights.
- Random vessel (p. 228). Roll a d10 for temperament:

| d10 | Temperament |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Negligible |
| 6–8 | Fleeting |
| 9–0 | Intense; roll again: 1–8 Intense, 9–0 Acute |

  Then roll a d10 for Resonance, unless the temperament came out negligible:

| d10 | Resonance |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Phlegmatic |
| 4–6 | Melancholy |
| 7–8 | Choleric |
| 9–0 | Sanguine |

  - **AMBIGUOUS:** the text says to roll for Resonance "if you get a 6+". That reads as the first temperament die. A negligible temperament still technically has a Resonance, it just has no effect.
- Animal blood never carries a Dyscrasia. Bagged blood has **no Resonance**.

### 2.6 Humanity, Stains, Convictions, Touchstones (pp. 172–174, 236–241)

- Humanity runs 0–10. Characters start at **7**, or **8** for fledglings. Predator types adjust it (p. 236).
  - **AMBIGUOUS:** p. 149 makes Humanity 8 for all-fledgling chronicles an ST option. p. 236 states it as the default for just-Embraced fledglings. Default: 7, with an ST toggle to 8.
- Track layout: Humanity fills boxes **left to right**. **Stains** fill boxes **right to left**.
- Stains are gained by breaking a chronicle **Tenet**:
  - Usually 1 for a justifiable breach, 2 or more for something bestial.
  - −1 or more if the breach served one of the character's **Convictions**.
  - Fixed amounts:

| Event | Stains |
|---|---|
| Blood Bonding a mortal | +1 |
| Embracing | +2 |
| Touchstone damaged | +1 |
| Touchstone damaged by your own actions | +2 |
| Touchstone destroyed | +2 |
| Touchstone destroyed by your own actions | +3 |

- **Degeneration:** when there are more Stains than empty boxes:
  - The character is **Impaired**: −2 to all pools.
  - They take **1 Aggravated Willpower per Stain that didn't fit**.
  - They can't knowingly break another Tenet. If forced to, they test for terror frenzy at Difficulty 4.
  - This lasts until the session ends, or until the player gives up 1 Humanity to clear the Stains (p. 239).
- **Remorse:** at the end of every session where the character has any Stains:
  - Roll dice equal to the **empty, unstained boxes**, minimum 1.
  - 1 or more successes: keep Humanity, clear all Stains.
  - 0 successes: **−1 Humanity**, then clear the Stains (p. 239).
- **Convictions:** 1–3 at creation. Each gets a **Touchstone** (a mortal). If a Touchstone is destroyed and the character can't move the attachment to someone else (a Humanity test at Difficulty 4), that Conviction is lost (p. 240).
- Ambition is a long-term goal; Desire is per session (p. 174).
- Humanity effects (pp. 236–241):
  - **Frenzy:** add **floor(Humanity / 3)** dice to frenzy resistance.
  - **Torpor length** by Humanity (table in JSON).
  - **Penalty to rolls interacting with humans:**

| Humanity | Penalty | With Blush of Life |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | −1 | — |
| 4 | −2 | — |
| 3 | −4 | — |
| 2 | −6 | −4 |
| 1 | −8 | −5 |

  - **Blush of Life and food:**
    - Humanity 10 and 9: no Blush needed.
    - Humanity 8: roll two dice on the Blush Rouse Check and keep the better.
    - Humanity 7 and below: normal.
    - Humanity 4: can't keep food down.
  - **Daytime:** waking during the day needs a Humanity roll. Once awake, the pool cap is the Humanity rating (p. 219).
  - **0 Humanity:** the character goes into the wassail and becomes a wight (an SPC). Their **Physical** Attributes become 5 for that scene (the errata limits this to Physical, and the core text already says Physical).
  - Errata 2.0 fixes the table's duplicated "Humanity 7" row to read Humanity 5. The table in hand already reads 9 through 1 correctly.
  - **AMBIGUOUS:** the torpor and frenzy table has no row for **Humanity 10**. Default: 3 frenzy dice (from the formula) and a 1-day torpor, extrapolated. Mark it as non-canon.
- **Buying Humanity with XP:** only at ST discretion, for extraordinary humane acts. Cost is **10 × the new rating** (p. 241).

### 2.7 Advantages: Merits, Backgrounds, Loresheets, Flaws (pp. 179–199)

- **Backgrounds** (p. 153): Allies, Contacts, Fame, Haven, Herd, Influence, Mask, Mawla, Resources, Retainers, Status (and Loresheets).
- **Merit/Flaw groups in the core** (pp. 179–183): Linguistics, Looks, Substance Use, Archaic, Bonding, Feeding, Mythic, and thin-blood Merits and Flaws.
  - Thin-blood Merits and Flaws have **no dot value**. Take 1–3 of each, in matching numbers. They don't count toward the Advantage or Flaw totals (p. 182).
- Advantage XP cost: **3 per dot**.

### 2.8 Predator type (pp. 175–178; hunting pools p. 307)

- Each type gives a **specialty** (pick one of two), **one Discipline dot** (pick one of two), and listed Advantages, Flaws, Humanity or BP changes. Full list in `v5.json`.
- If the specialty is in a Skill with 0 dots, the character gets **one dot in that Skill instead** (p. 149).
- Thin-bloods, and many fledglings, don't pick a predator type (p. 149).
- Restrictions:
  - **Ventrue** can't be Bagger or Farmer.
  - **Farmer** requires BP below 3.
  - The "Tremere only" Blood Sorcery option exists for Bagger and Osiris.
- **Farmer conflict:**
  - The core text in hand says "Folkloric banes include: (••) Vegan" for Farmer (p. 177).
  - Errata 2.0 tells you to change "Gain the Feeding Flaw: (••) Vegan" into "Folkloric banes include:". It also tells you to change the p. 181 Feeding Flaw "Vegan" to "Farmer". p. 181 in hand already lists "Flaw (••) Farmer".
  - The p. 177 wording is an obvious misapplied errata line. **Implement Farmer as granting Feeding Flaw (••) Farmer.** Flag it as an errata conflict.

## 3. Vampire mechanics

### 3.1 Hunger gain and loss

- **Gain:** a failed Rouse Check (+1). Some bestial failures (+1). Being fed on by another vampire (+1 per point the drinker slakes).
- **Slaking** (feeding table, p. 212):

| Source | Hunger slaked | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Several small animals | 1 | a scene | nothing above BP 2 |
| Medium animal | 1 | a turn | |
| Large animal | 2 | a scene | |
| Blood bag | 1 | a turn | nothing above BP 2; no Resonance |
| Sip from a human | 1 | 3 turns | |
| Safe maximum from a human | 2 | a scene | |
| Harmful drink from a human | 1–4 | 1 turn per point | Aggravated damage to the human = Hunger slaked; the human rolls Strength + Stamina vs that number to survive |
| Drain and kill a human | 5 | 5 turns | **the only way to reach Hunger 0** |

- Blood Potency limits the **minimum Hunger** without a kill (§3.4) and lowers the yield from humans and animals.
- **AMBIGUOUS:** p. 211 says "above BP 7, Hunger can't go below 3 without killing". The BP table puts the drop to 3 at BP 9 (BP 5 through 8 → 2; BP 9–10 → 3). **Follow the table.**
- **Feeding from vampires** (p. 213): 1 Hunger slaked for each Hunger point the donor gains.
  - Donor at least 2 BP higher: 2 slaked per point.
  - Donor at least 2 BP lower: 1 slaked per 2 points.
  - It risks a Blood Bond.
- **Bite attack** (p. 213, updated by Errata 2.0; the core in hand already has it):
  - Declared before rolling. Either win a grapple, or make a Strength + Brawl called shot at a 1-success penalty.
  - A hit deals exactly **2 Aggravated** damage.
  - Each turn of feeding deals **1 Aggravated** to a mortal and slakes 1 Hunger.

### 3.2 Compulsions (pp. 208–211)

- Triggered by a bestial failure. The ST picks one, or rolls:

| d10 | Compulsion |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Hunger |
| 4–5 | Dominance |
| 6–7 | Harm |
| 8–9 | Paranoia |
| 0 | Clan Compulsion (re-roll for Caitiff or thin-bloods) |

- Most impose **−2 dice** on everything that doesn't serve the urge, until it's resolved or the scene ends.
- **Willpower can't be spent to ignore a Compulsion.** Playing one out well can restore Willpower.
- Clan Compulsions:

| Clan | Compulsion | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Brujah | Rebellion | −2 dice on all rolls until the character has opposed authority or expectations |
| Gangrel | Feral Impulses | **−3** dice to Manipulation and Intelligence rolls for one scene; one-word speech only |
| Malkavian | Delusion | −2 dice to Dexterity, Manipulation, Composure and Wits rolls, and to resisting terror frenzy, for one scene |
| Nosferatu | Cryptophilia | −2 dice to anything that isn't about learning a secret |
| Toreador | Obsession | −2 dice to anything that isn't about the object of beauty |
| Tremere | Perfectionism | −2 dice until a critical win on a Skill roll or the scene ends; −1 on the first repeat of a task and none on the second |
| Ventrue | Arrogance | −2 dice to anything that isn't leadership, until an order is obeyed (not one enforced by powers) |

- Malkavian bane add-on: whenever they suffer a bestial failure or a Compulsion, they also take a penalty equal to their Bane Severity to one category of pools for the scene.

| Neonates | 12th–13th |
| Ancillae | 10th–11th |

| Generation | BP |
|---|---|
| 14th–16th | 0 |
| 12th–13th | 1 |
| 10th–11th | 2 |

- **BP limits by generation** (p. 214):

| Generation | Min BP | Max BP |
|---|---|---|
| 4th | 5 | 10 |
| 5th | 4 | 9 |

### 3.4 Blood Potency table (core p. 216)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 1 | +1 | 1 | — | 1 | 1 | none |
| 2 | +1 | 2 | +1 | 1 | 1 | animal and bagged blood slake half |
| 3 | +2 | 2 | +1 | ≤2 | 2 | animal and bagged blood slake nothing |
| 4 | +2 | 3 | +2 | ≤2 | 2 | as BP 3; −1 slaked per human |
| 5 | +3 | 3 | +2 | ≤3 | 3 | as BP 4; must kill to go below Hunger 2 |

| 10 | +5 | 5 | +5 | ≤5 | 5 | animal and bagged blood slake nothing; −3 slaked per human; must kill to go below 3 |

- **AMBIGUOUS:** the BP 6 and BP 8 rows list only the animal and bagged blood line. Read literally, they'd have *smaller* human penalties than BP 5 and BP 7. That's almost certainly an omission. Recommended default: **inherit from the row below**. BP 6 = −1 per human and must kill to go below 2. BP 8 = −2 per human and must kill to go below 2. The JSON carries both the literal table and the default.
- Errata 2.0 (p. 244): the BP Discipline bonus, and Intense Temperament, only apply to pools that use the Discipline as a Trait or that a Discipline power directly adds dice to. **Count it once**, even when several Disciplines boost the same roll.
- **Bane Severity** = the BP column. Thin-bloods are 0.
- BP 6 and above isn't meant for player characters (p. 217).

### 3.5 Blood gifts (pp. 218–219)

- **Blush of Life:** 1 Rouse Check. The vampire looks alive for the night.
- **Blood Surge:** 1 Rouse Check.
  - Adds BP-based dice to one roll that uses an Attribute. Once per roll.
  - Not usable on Willpower or Humanity rolls, multi-scene rolls, or One-Roll Conflict.
  - Automatic wins and "taking half" don't apply to a surged roll.
- **Mend Superficial:** 1 Rouse per turn, healing the number of points in the BP table.
- **Mend Aggravated:** at the next nightfall, **3 Rouse Checks** on top of the waking Rouse.
  - Each set heals **1 Aggravated point** and one crippling injury. Only one Aggravated point can be healed per night.
  - If those Rouses push Hunger above 5, the vampire goes into **torpor** (no frenzy test).
  - p. 127 says vampires mend "1 level of Aggravated Health damage each night by Rousing the Blood", which matches.
- **Disciplines:** each activation costs what the power lists, usually 1 Rouse. One activation per turn.
- In one turn a vampire could use a power, Blush, Surge and Superficial mending. That costs at least 3 Rouses plus the power's own cost (p. 219).

### 3.6 Waking and day (p. 219)

- **1 Rouse Check every sunset.** If it fails at Hunger 5, the vampire stays in **torpor** instead of testing for frenzy.
- **Waking during the day:** a Humanity roll.

| Situation | Difficulty |
|---|---|
| Fire or other danger to life | 3 |
| Urgent message | 4 |
| Inconvenience | 5+ |

  - It lasts one scene. Each additional scene needs a Humanity roll at Difficulty 3. A critical win means the vampire stays up as long as needed.
  - The pool cap is the Humanity rating.

### 3.7 Frenzy (pp. 219–220)

- **Resist:** roll **Willpower** (current undamaged) + floor(Humanity / 3) against a Difficulty the ST sets.
  - Win: resisted, but the character spends their turn suppressing it.
  - **Critical win:** resisted at no cost.
- **Riding the Wave:** the player chooses to frenzy without rolling and keeps control of the character.
- **In frenzy:**
  - Ignore Health penalties short of mutilation.
  - Only physical Disciplines can be used.
  - **+3 dice** to resist mental Disciplines. If the power has no resistance pool, the user takes +2 Difficulty.
  - Willpower: 1 point = control for one turn. **No Willpower re-rolls.**
  - Can't be pushed into a second frenzy, and gets no Compulsions.
  - Lasts until the goal is met or the scene ends.
- **Fury frenzy:**
  - Triggers: friend killed 2; lover or Touchstone hurt 3; lover or Touchstone killed 4; physical provocation 2; insulted by an inferior 2; public humiliation 2.
  - Ending it: after destroying the target, a Willpower test at Difficulty 3, or 5 if enemies remain.
- **Hunger frenzy:**
  - Triggers: sight or smell of blood at Hunger 4+ is 2; taste of blood at Hunger 4+ is 3; failing a Rouse at Hunger 5 is 4.
  - It ends at **Hunger 1 or less**.
- **Terror frenzy (Rötschreck):**
  - Triggers: bonfire 2; burning building 3; being burned 2; obscured sunlight 3; full direct sunlight 4.
  - It ends when no danger can be perceived, or when the scene ends.
- Clan bane modifiers:
  - Brujah: −Bane Severity dice to resist **fury** frenzy, minimum 1 die.
  - Malkavian Delusion: −2 to resist terror frenzy.
- Thin-bloods frenzy **only through supernatural means**, unless they have the Bestial Temper Flaw (pp. 215, 182).

### 3.8 Sunlight, fire, torpor, Final Death (pp. 221–223)

- **Direct sun:** **Aggravated damage per turn equal to Bane Severity.** Obscured sun or protective clothing: that much every other turn, or less.
- **Thin-bloods:** 1 **Superficial** per turn.
- **Fire:** Aggravated, by how much of the body is exposed (ST call; 1 for a hand, 3 or more when engulfed).
- **Stake through the heart** (called shot at −2, 5+ damage before halving): paralysis.
- **Decapitation** (called shot at −2, beheading weapon, 10+ damage before halving): Final Death.
- **Torpor:**
  - Entered at Health full of Aggravated, at a failed waking Rouse at Hunger 5, or voluntarily.
  - Duration is set by Humanity. The vampire wakes at **Hunger 5**.
  - A vampire in torpor from damage tries to mend 1 Health per night.
  - **Any further Aggravated damage from fire or sun** while in torpor with Health full of Aggravated → **Final Death**.

## 4. Character creation (core pp. 135–154; Errata 2.0 applied)

1. **Concept, chronicle, sect.** Pick generation according to the coterie's age (§3.3).
2. **Clan.** One of 7 clans, Caitiff, or thin-blood.
   - Thin-bloods get no clan and no Disciplines. They take 1–3 thin-blood Merits and the same number of thin-blood Flaws (p. 142).
3. **Attributes** (p. 144, with the errata wording, which the core in hand already includes):
   - **One at 4, three at 3, four at 2, one at 1.** That's 22 dots in total.
   - The pre-errata text read "four at 3, three at 2". **Don't use it.**
4. **Derived Traits:** Health = Stamina + 3. Willpower = Composure + Resolve.
5. **Skills.** Choose one distribution:

| Distribution | Spread |
|---|---|
| Jack of all trades | one at 3, eight at 2, ten at 1 |
| Balanced | three at 3, five at 2, seven at 1 |
| Specialist | one at 4, three at 3, three at 2, three at 1 |

   - Then add the **free specialties**: one each for Academics, Craft, Performance and Science (if the character has dots in them), plus **one more of the player's choice**. The predator type adds another.
   - The book also gives a longer "life path" method (pp. 145–146): profession (two Skills at 3, two at 2, one professional specialty), life events (one at 3, one at 2), leisure (three at 1), and an extra pick (Specialist: one Skill at 4; or Generalist: two at 2 and four at 1).
   - **AMBIGUOUS:** that method doesn't say what happens when two steps land on the same Skill. Default for the app: offer only the three quick distributions, which is what p. 147 calls the alternative.
6. **Disciplines:** pick **two clan Disciplines**, **2 dots in one and 1 dot in the other**, with one power per dot (p. 149).
   - Caitiff: any two Disciplines, 2 and 1.
   - **AMBIGUOUS:** the Caitiff clan text (p. 107) says "three Disciplines of your choice", which conflicts with the 2 + 1 rule on p. 149. Default: 2 + 1 (the creation procedure). The p. 107 line is about which Disciplines count as available.
7. **Predator type:** a specialty, +1 Discipline dot, and the type's extras (§2.8).
8. **Convictions** 1–3, each with a **Touchstone**. Ambition. Current Desire.
9. **Humanity 7.** Fledgling option 8. Adjusted by predator type.
10. **Blood Potency** from generation (§3.3). Adjusted by predator type (Blood Leech +1).
11. **Advantages: up to 7 dots** of Merits and Backgrounds. **At least 2 dots of Flaws** (p. 150).
    - **AMBIGUOUS:** the core gives **no maximum** for Flaws in vampire creation. The "maximum Flaws" figures on p. 185 belong to the Retainers stat templates (mortal servants), not to player characters. Default: minimum 2 and no hard maximum, with an optional ST cap.
    - Flaws given by the predator type: whether they count toward the 2-point minimum isn't stated. Default: they count.
12. **Loresheet** (optional, uses Advantage dots).
13. **Coterie:** **1 Coterie dot per player** (2 in small troupes), pooled with leftover Advantage dots for Domain and shared Backgrounds.
14. **Sea of Time** (p. 151), by age:

| Age | Extras |
|---|---|
| Childer / fledglings | nothing |
| Neonates | **15 XP** |
| Ancillae | **+1 BP**, +2 Advantage dots, +2 Flaw dots, **−1 Humanity**, **35 XP** |

   - **AMBIGUOUS:** ancillae are 10th–11th generation, which already gives starting BP 2 (p. 149). Read literally, the +1 makes BP 3 (still within the 10th–11th maximum of 4). Some tables read it as "BP 2 total". Default: **literal, BP 3**. Make it configurable.

**XP awards** (p. 130): 1 per session plus 1 at the end of each story. Optional: 2 per session for fast games.

**XP costs** (p. 151; "new level" = the rating being bought):

| Purchase | XP cost |
|---|---|

## Differences that matter for the app

- **Difficulty means different things.** V5: the number of successes needed, with a fixed per-die target of 6. Classic: the per-die target number. The roll API needs a ruleset discriminator and should never reuse the `difficulty` field across the two.
- **No ones and no botch** in V5. 1s only matter on **Hunger dice**, and only on failed rolls (bestial failure). Criticals are **pairs of 10s worth 4**, not open-ended rerolls or double successes.
- **Hunger dice** are a separate category inside the same pool. The roll result must keep regular and Hunger dice apart so it can work out messy criticals and bestial failures and restrict Willpower re-rolls.
- **Willpower** re-rolls up to 3 regular dice and costs Superficial Willpower damage. Classic Willpower buys +1 automatic success.
- **Specialties:** +1 die, and only one per roll.
- **Rouse Checks** are single-die checks that change **Hunger**. Classic spends **blood points** from a pool sized by generation. V5 has **no blood pool**.
- **Trackers scale with Attributes:** Health = Stamina + 3, Willpower = Composure + Resolve. There are two damage types, Superficial (halved, rounded up) and Aggravated. The only penalty is Impairment (−2) once a tracker is full. Classic has 7 fixed wound levels with graded penalties and soak rolls; V5 has **no soak roll**.
- **Contests:** the actor wins ties, and damage = margin + weapon modifier. Classic is a resisted roll where the opponent's successes cancel yours, plus a separate damage roll and soak roll.
- **Morality:** Humanity with **Stains and a Remorse roll** at the end of each session, Convictions and Touchstones. Classic uses Virtue-based degeneration rolls (Conscience at difficulty 8).
- **Generation → Blood Potency.** BP drives Surge, mending, Discipline bonus, Rouse re-rolls, Bane Severity and feeding limits. Bane Severity scales the clan banes.
- **Creation:** fixed Attribute spread (4/3/3/3/2/2/2/2/1), Skill distribution templates, 2 + 1 Discipline dots, 7 Advantage dots and at least 2 Flaw dots, a predator type, and age-based XP. There are no freebie points. XP costs use the **new** level.
- **Clans:** V5 core has 7 clans plus Caitiff and thin-bloods. The other classic clans (Assamite/Banu Haqim, Lasombra and others) aren't in this book, so don't offer them for V5 without a supplement.
