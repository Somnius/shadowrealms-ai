# ShadowRealms AI v0.9 screenshots

Taken from the production build (nginx, `http://localhost`) on 2026-10-04. Desktop shots were rendered at 1440x900 (2x) and scaled to 1600px wide. Mobile shots were rendered at 390x844 (3x) and scaled to 780px wide. Everything is WebP, which was 4 to 15 times smaller than PNG.

The chats, rolls and Storyteller replies come from the demo chronicles "Athens by Night" (V5) and "Constantinople Nights" (Revised). The Storyteller text is real AI output (LM Studio, llama-krikri-8b-instruct), saved exactly as returned. In the hall and play shots, the test chronicles were hidden in the page itself before capture. Nothing was deleted.

## Sign-in

- `login-desktop.webp`: sign-in page with the animated sigil and fog, after the animation has settled.
- `login-mobile.webp`: the same page on a phone (390px).
- `register.webp`: the Register tab with the live password rules (some met, some not yet).

## Hall and play

- `hall.webp`: the chronicle hall with a Classic chronicle and a V5 chronicle, edition badges and "You play" rows.
- `play-v5.webp`: Athens by Night, Elysium. Two dice cards (a roll and its Willpower reroll), a Rouse check line, an AI Storyteller reply, the player's next line, and the right panel (character, Hunger, Storyteller, staff tools).
- `play-greek.webp`: the same chronicle with the UI in Greek (Plaka Rooftops), showing a Greek in-character line, a Greek Storyteller reply and a dice card.
- `atmosphere-menu.webp`: the account menu with Language (English/Ελληνικά) and Atmosphere (Full / Subtle / Off).
